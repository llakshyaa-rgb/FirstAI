REAL-WORLD DATA EXAMPLES — INDIA
Source: World Bank World Development Indicators.
Snapshot checked: 2026-11-14.
These are published annual statistical estimates, not live measurements. Preserve the year and unit. Missing values must never be interpreted as zero.

Question: What was India's population in 2025 according to the World Bank?
Answer: The World Bank reports India's 2025 population as 1,463,865,525 people (about 1.464 billion). This is an annual population estimate, not a live headcount.
Indicator: Population, total (SP.POP.TOTL). Unit: people. Year: 2025.
Source: https://databank.worldbank.org/reports.aspx?country=IND&source=2

Question: What was India's GDP in current US dollars in 2025?
Answer: The World Bank reports India's 2025 GDP at approximately USD 3,956,067.12 million (about USD 3.956 trillion) in current US dollars. Current-dollar GDP is not inflation-adjusted.
Indicator: GDP (current US$) (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD). Unit: current US dollars. Year: 2025.
Source: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD?locations=IN

Question: What was India's life expectancy at birth in 2024?
Answer: The World Bank reports India's life expectancy at birth at 72.235 years for 2024. This is a population-level statistical indicator, not a prediction of an individual's lifespan.
Indicator: Life expectancy at birth, total (SP.DYN.LE00.IN). Unit: years. Year: 2024.
Source: https://data.worldbank.org/country/in

DATA QUALITY RULES
- Always attach the year and unit to a statistic.
- Distinguish annual estimates from real-time observations and forecasts.
- Do not describe current US dollar GDP as inflation-adjusted GDP.
- Do not treat missing values as zero.
- Do not infer causation from descriptive indicators.
- Re-check source data before answering time-sensitive questions.
